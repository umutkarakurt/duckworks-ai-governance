## No-duplication principle

Different assessments answer different questions:

- **AIA** — broad positive/adverse business, people, governance, reliability, security, accessibility, and other impacts.
- **Ethical assessment** — human agency, dignity, fairness, transparency, accountability, societal/workforce effects, and ethical trade-offs.
- **DPIA** — personal-data processing risks and GDPR Article 35 analysis where applicable.
- **FRIA** — fundamental-rights analysis; statutory applicability must be determined separately.
- **HUDERIA** — Council of Europe human-rights, democracy, and rule-of-law methodology used here as non-binding governance guidance.
- **Risk assessment** — scenario-based likelihood/severity, controls, residual risk, and treatment.

These assessments should cross-reference shared facts rather than create conflicting system descriptions or scores.
