## Boundary

HUDERIA is treated here as **standalone, non-legally binding guidance**. The Council of Europe Framework Convention has its own treaty and domestic-law applicability questions; this pack does not assert that the Convention directly requires these Duckworks documents.

## Current decision

The HUDERIA work does not clear DuckTalent for use. The current gate remains **DO NOT DEPLOY**. Blocking controls require evidence before they can be considered implemented.

No real stakeholder engagement, real applicant testing, or real-world impact measurement should be inferred from portfolio plans or synthetic records.
