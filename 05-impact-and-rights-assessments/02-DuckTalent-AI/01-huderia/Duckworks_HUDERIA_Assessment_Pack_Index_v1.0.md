## 1. Purpose

This pack translates the Council of Europe HUDERIA Methodology and the 2026 HUDERIA Model: Context-Based Risk Analysis (COBRA) Resources into a practical Duckworks evidence set. It is structured to complement, rather than replace, the existing Duckworks AIA, EU FRIA, GDPR DPIA, enterprise risk assessment, security review, and lifecycle governance records.

| **Status of HUDERIA.** HUDERIA is standalone, non-legally binding guidance. It may be used by public and private actors and can be adapted to existing governance approaches. Duckworks therefore treats this pack as recommended organizational practice, not as a legal requirement created by HUDERIA itself. |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## 6. Legal and Framework Boundary

The Council of Europe Framework Convention is a legally binding treaty for its Parties. Its requirements are implemented through Party-level legal and policy measures; the treatment of private actors depends on the applicable Party approach and domestic law. This pack therefore does not assert that the Convention directly imposes these documents on Duckworks. Legal applicability must be confirmed separately by Duckworks Legal.
