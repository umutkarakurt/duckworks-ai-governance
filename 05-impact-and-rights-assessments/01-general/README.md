## Purpose

The assessment evaluates ethical implications through human-centred, fairness, transparency, safety, privacy, well-being, and accountability lenses. It is designed to ask who benefits, who bears risk, where power or information asymmetry exists, whether people retain meaningful agency, and whether adverse effects can be challenged or remedied.

The ethical priority scale is a **qualitative Duckworks governance judgment**, not a legal classification or the 5×5 enterprise risk score.

## Relationship to other assessments

This assessment complements:

- system AI impact assessments;
- enterprise risk assessments;
- legal/regulatory triage;
- GDPR DPIAs;
- fundamental-rights/HUDERIA work;
- security and safety validation.

It should reuse established system facts rather than invent new implementation details. Ethical significance can differ from legal status or quantitative risk and should remain separately labelled.
