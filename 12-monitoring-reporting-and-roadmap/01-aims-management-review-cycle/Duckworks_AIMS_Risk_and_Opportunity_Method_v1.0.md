# Duckworks AIMS Risk and Opportunity Method

**Document ID:** DW-AIMS-RO-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 8 September 2026  
**Owner:** AI Governance Lead  
**Approval status:** Approved within synthetic portfolio exercise only

## 1. Purpose

This method governs risks and opportunities affecting the ability of the proposed Duckworks AI Management System to achieve its intended outcomes. It complements, but does not replace, system-level AI risk assessments.

## 2. Classification

- **AIMS risk:** an uncertain event or condition that may impair governance scope, competence, evidence, oversight, monitoring, supplier control, assurance or improvement.
- **AIMS opportunity:** a feasible improvement that may increase governance coverage, efficiency, auditability or decision quality.
- System harms remain in the canonical AI risk register. The AIMS register records management-system failure or improvement conditions.

## 3. Scoring

Risks use likelihood and impact scores from 1 to 5. Inherent and current scores equal likelihood × impact.

| Score | Rating | Required response |
|---:|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low | Monitor through routine review |
| 5–9 | Moderate | Assign owner and proportionate action |
| 10–15 | High | Management-review visibility and dated treatment required |
| 16–25 | Critical | Immediate escalation and explicit lifecycle/resource decision required |

Opportunities use feasibility and value scores from 1 to 5. Their product is a prioritization score, not a reduction in risk. Risk and opportunity scores must not be netted against one another.

## 4. Required fields

Each entry records ID, type, cause, event or opportunity, consequence/value, scoring basis, existing response, current position, treatment, owner, due date, status, linked objectives, evidence IDs, triggers and boundary.

## 5. Review and escalation

The register is reviewed at least quarterly, at AIMS management review, and after material changes to scope, obligations, evidence, competence, suppliers, incidents, audits or objectives. High/Critical risks, overdue treatments and blocked dependencies require management visibility. Only authorized humans may accept risk, change objectives, authorize resources or close actions.

## 6. Evidence boundary

This is a synthetic portfolio method. Its scores support prioritization within the fictional scenario and do not establish probability, legal exposure, production loss, compliance, conformity or certification.
