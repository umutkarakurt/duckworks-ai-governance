## AI Vendor Contract Template

The vendor template separates:

- **CORE** Duckworks organizational requirements;
- **ENHANCED** requirements for higher-impact or more sensitive suppliers;
- **CONDITIONAL LEGAL** provisions that apply only when the relevant legal trigger and roles are confirmed;
- **OPTIONAL** commercial/use-case-specific provisions.

This prevents Duckworks internal risk tiers from being misrepresented as legal classifications.

## AI DPA Template

The DPA is primarily structured for a GDPR controller-to-processor relationship. It explicitly requires actual role analysis rather than assuming that contract labels determine controller/processor status.

## Important boundary

These documents are fictional portfolio templates, not executed agreements and not legal advice. Real use would require verification of the parties, processing facts, jurisdiction, transfers, national law, liability position, service architecture, and other applicable terms.

Contractual allocation can manage risk but does not transfer Duckworks' governance accountability.
