# Project W.I.N.G. Closeout and Forward Roadmap v1.0

## Closeout position

Project W.I.N.G. has reached a coherent portfolio baseline through v1.8. The repository now demonstrates governance design, three operating-evidence archetypes, technical control testing, supplier governance, material-change response, corrective action, management review, control applicability, internal-audit planning and executive decision support.

The portfolio should now be treated as a stable case-study product. Further expansion should be driven by a defined evidence gap, external evaluator feedback or a target-role requirement—not by document volume.

## Outstanding credibility gaps

### F-014 — Approval claims without real authority artifacts

**Status:** Open by design.  
**Treatment:** Continue labelling all committee, executive and approval records as synthetic. Do not replace the limitation with invented signatures or approvals.  
**External validation route:** Obtain permission to present a sanitized real decision artifact, or demonstrate the method in a supervised case exercise.

### F-033 — Design-heavy versus operation-light maturity

**Status:** Partially addressed.  
**Treatment:** The three worked evidence archetypes and management-system gates demonstrate operating-model thinking. They do not establish sustained production operation.  
**External validation route:** Add a real, non-confidential control-operation example only when lawful and authorized.

### F-044 — Recurring management-reporting evidence

**Status:** Partially addressed.  
**Treatment:** The executive brief and bounded AIMS review cycle show the reporting design and one synthetic follow-up cycle.  
**External validation route:** Demonstrate periodic reporting through an authorized role, case exercise or future project with successive time periods.

## Thirty-day hiring-conversion plan

| Period | Action | Output | Success measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–5 | Test the five-minute review with two independent reviewers | Consolidated feedback log | Both reviewers identify the three strongest evidence chains without guidance |
| Days 6–10 | Rehearse the six interview cases | Recorded or written responses | Each answer stays under two minutes and states its limitation |
| Days 11–15 | Tailor the competency map to three target job descriptions | Three filtered extracts | Every claimed competency links to inspectable evidence |
| Days 16–20 | Publish one concise portfolio case study | LinkedIn or application excerpt | At least one substantive response from a relevant practitioner or recruiter |
| Days 21–25 | Run a skeptical interview review | Challenge log and revised answers | No unsupported production, compliance or certification claim remains |
| Days 26–30 | Review conversion outcomes | Short decision record | Decide whether to hold the baseline or make one targeted enhancement |

## Change rule after v1.8

A proposed portfolio change should proceed only when it meets at least one condition:

1. closes a documented evidence or consistency gap;
2. responds to specific evaluator or interview feedback;
3. demonstrates a capability required by a named target role; or
4. corrects a factual, traceability, reproducibility or accessibility defect.

Changes that merely add another template, repeat an existing capability or imply unsupported maturity should be rejected.
