## Archive rules

Artifacts in this folder are **not current authoritative project records** unless a file explicitly states otherwise.

Archived material may be retained to demonstrate:

- methodology evolution;
- version history;
- why a scoring or governance approach changed;
- traceability from draft to approved baseline;
- evidence that superseded conclusions were not silently deleted.

## Reviewer caution

Do not use archived risk scores, control conclusions, or draft methods as the current Duckworks position.

The active v1.0 methodology and baseline risk scenario register are maintained under `04-risk-assessment/`.

A mature document-control process would also record superseded-by relationships, version status, and effective dates in a controlled document register.
