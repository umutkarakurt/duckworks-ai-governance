## Governance architecture

The operating model is designed around:

- executive sponsorship and risk appetite;
- accountable first-line business/system ownership;
- second-line AI Governance, Risk, Security, Privacy, Legal, HR, and specialist challenge;
- cross-functional AI Governance Committee decision rights;
- independent third-line Internal Audit;
- affected-stakeholder input for material human impacts;
- lifecycle gates from intake through approval, monitoring, significant change, suspension, and retirement.

## Important separation of duties

Internal Audit may evaluate design and operating effectiveness but should not own or operate controls it later audits.

Business/system owners remain accountable for intended purpose, operating controls, treatment, and evidence; specialist functions provide review and challenge rather than silently inheriting business ownership.

## How this folder connects

The operating model consumes inputs from inventory, risk, impact, legal/privacy/security review, and controls. Its outputs include approval decisions, conditions, escalations, exceptions, reassessment requirements, and evidence responsibilities.
