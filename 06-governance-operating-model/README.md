## AIMS objectives and support package

The v1.4 package demonstrates a bounded management-system support cycle:

**Objective → metric and target → formula status → management response → competence criteria → authorization gate → exception → controlled evidence**

It includes eight objectives, a six-sheet performance workbook, three synthetic competence records, a deterministic authorization gate, one expired and one below-threshold blocked case, and a fourteen-record priority document register. The gate grants zero authorizations and requires a human authorization decision.

See: [`01-aims-objectives-and-support/`](01-aims-objectives-and-support/)

The downstream [`AIMS risk, opportunity and management-review cycle`](../12-monitoring-reporting-and-roadmap/01-aims-management-review-cycle/) consumes these objectives and support records, records management decisions and assigned actions, and tests overdue/dependency escalation without granting authority or closing actions.

## Governance architecture

The operating model is designed around:

- executive sponsorship and risk appetite;
- accountable first-line business/system ownership;
- second-line AI Governance, Risk, Security, Privacy, Legal, HR, and specialist challenge;
- cross-functional AI Governance Committee decision rights;
- independent third-line Internal Audit;
- affected-stakeholder input for material human impacts;
- lifecycle gates from intake through approval, monitoring, significant change, suspension, and retirement.

## Important separation of duties

Internal Audit may evaluate design and operating effectiveness but should not own or operate controls it later audits.

Business/system owners remain accountable for intended purpose, operating controls, treatment, and evidence; specialist functions provide review and challenge rather than silently inheriting business ownership.

## How this folder connects

The operating model consumes inputs from inventory, risk, impact, legal/privacy/security review, controls, objectives, competence records, and documented information. Its outputs include approval decisions, conditions, escalations, exceptions, reassessment requirements, authorization eligibility, management responses, and evidence responsibilities.
