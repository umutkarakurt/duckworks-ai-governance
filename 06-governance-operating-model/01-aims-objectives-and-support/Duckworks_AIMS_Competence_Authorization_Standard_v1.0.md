## 1. Control objective

Only people who meet defined role criteria, complete required learning, pass assessment and hold current scope-specific authorization may perform controlled AIMS activities. Automated eligibility checks may block progression but cannot grant authority.

## 2. Minimum record

Each controlled role requires:

- role and competence profile;
- required learning and assessment threshold;
- dated completion and assessment result;
- authorization scope, approver, start and expiry date;
- independence or conflict requirements where applicable;
- refresher and reassessment triggers;
- restriction, suspension or revocation status; and
- retained evidence ID.

## 3. Authorization conditions

A record is eligible only when all conditions are true:

1. required learning is complete;
2. assessment score meets or exceeds the role threshold;
3. the assessment and authorization have not expired;
4. authorization scope matches the requested controlled activity;
5. no suspension, conflict or overdue mandatory refresher is active; and
6. the designated human authority approves the authorization.

## 4. Triggers for reassessment

- material role, system, model, data, supplier or legal-context change;
- control failure, incident or repeated procedural error;
- lapse or expiry of authorization;
- failed knowledge or practical assessment;
- extended absence from the controlled activity; or
- audit or management-review finding.

## 5. Segregation and limits

- Internal Audit competence does not authorize control ownership.
- AI Governance coordination does not authorize business risk acceptance.
- Technical expertise does not replace Legal, Privacy, Security, Safety or affected-domain challenge.
- An eligibility result is advisory and must never be interpreted as automatic appointment or authorization.
