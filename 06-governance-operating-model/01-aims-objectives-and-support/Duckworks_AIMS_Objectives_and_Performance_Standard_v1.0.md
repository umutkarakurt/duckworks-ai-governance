## 1. Purpose

This standard defines how Duckworks establishes, measures, reviews and changes AIMS objectives. It converts broad governance intentions into accountable, measurable plans without treating one metric as proof of safety, compliance or control effectiveness.

## 2. Required objective fields

Every objective must identify:

1. a stable objective and metric ID;
2. a defined population and measurement period;
3. a dated baseline and current value;
4. a target and target date;
5. an accountable owner and data steward;
6. a review cadence and source record;
7. a response if the target is missed; and
8. an evidence boundary describing what the metric cannot establish.

## 3. Status logic

| Status | Meaning | Required response |
|---|---|---|
| Met | Current value satisfies the approved target within the defined scope | Preserve evidence and continue monitoring |
| At risk | Target is not yet met but corrective action remains feasible before the target date | Record owner, action and next review |
| Blocked | A condition prevents progression or would make progression unacceptable | Preserve the relevant lifecycle gate and escalate |
| Not measured | Source, population or method is not sufficiently defined | Do not infer performance; establish the measurement basis |

## 4. Review and escalation

- The AI Governance Lead updates the register monthly and before AIMS-wide management review.
- Metric owners attest to source completeness and explain exclusions.
- High/Critical risk, missed gate-compliance, expired authorization, overdue reassessment, stale evidence or unresolved supplier blockers are escalated to the AI Governance Committee.
- Targets may be changed only with a recorded rationale, approver and effective date. A target must not be relaxed solely to turn a failed metric green.
- Material metric-definition changes require a new version and preservation of the prior calculation basis.

## 5. Evidence interpretation

The v1.0 dashboard is a synthetic portfolio snapshot. A green indicator means only that the stated target is met for its defined synthetic population. It does not establish production effectiveness, legal compliance, absence of harm or ISO/IEC 42001 conformity.
