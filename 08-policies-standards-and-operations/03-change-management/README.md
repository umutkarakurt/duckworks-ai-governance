## Purpose

The process is designed to prevent a material AI change from silently invalidating earlier risk, impact, legal, privacy, security, safety, human-oversight, vendor, or approval conclusions.

It covers changes such as:

- model/provider/version changes;
- data, retrieval, or knowledge-source changes;
- configuration and permission changes;
- integrations and third-party changes;
- intended-purpose or autonomy changes;
- material operating-context changes.

## Process components

The pack defines change request, classification/impact screening, reassessment and control-impact checks, approval/release gates, emergency change, third-party model/vendor change, rollback/suspension/recovery, and a change/evidence register.

## Important terminology

“Material AI change” is a **Duckworks internal governance term**. It is not automatically equivalent to statutory concepts such as “substantial modification.” Where a change may alter legal role or regulatory classification, Legal/Compliance must assess that separately.

## Evidence principle

No material AI change should be released without impact screening, accountable approval, and traceable evidence.
