## Purpose

The plan defines how Duckworks identifies, sources, classifies, documents, prepares, accesses, shares, validates, monitors, retains, and disposes of data used by or generated through AI systems.

Its scope includes, where relevant:

- training, validation, test, and challenge data;
- input data;
- RAG / vector-store / knowledge-base sources;
- prompts and uploaded files;
- AI outputs, rankings, forecasts, defect flags, and embeddings;
- labels, features, metadata, and quality records;
- security, oversight, audit, and performance logs;
- third-party data dependencies;
- retention, deletion, archival, and exit evidence.

## Core data principle

**No material AI dataset without purpose, owner, and provenance; no sensitive data without an approved handling basis; no material data change without traceable reassessment.**

## Legal boundary

GDPR and EU AI Act data requirements are applied only when their actual scope and role conditions are met. The plan does not turn every AI dataset into personal data or every Duckworks system into a legally high-risk AI system.
