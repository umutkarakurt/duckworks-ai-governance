# Policies, Standards and Operations

**Repository path:** `08-policies-standards-and-operations/`  
**Status:** Portfolio baseline with operational design artifacts  

[← Back to main portfolio](../README.md)

This folder converts governance principles and control requirements into organizational rules, data practices, change processes, and repeatable operational actions.

## Subfolders

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `01-policies/` | Enterprise AI governance, responsible use, and protected reporting requirements. |
| `02-data-governance/` | AI data lifecycle, provenance, quality, access, RAG/retrieval, retention, and evidence. |
| `03-change-management/` | Controlled material change, reassessment, release, rollback, and evidence traceability. |
| `04-operational-guidance/` | Trigger-driven playbooks and runbooks for day-to-day governance execution. |

## Policy hierarchy

The intended hierarchy is:

**Governance Charter → AI Governance Policy → domain policies/standards → lifecycle SOP → playbooks/runbooks/templates → operating evidence**

This folder should be read together with `06-governance-operating-model/` and `07-control-framework/`.

## Important boundary

These artifacts may define mandatory **Duckworks internal requirements** once approved, but internal rules are not automatically legal requirements. Applicable law must be identified separately, and a policy cannot authorize unlawful activity.

Operational procedures demonstrate process design; they do not prove that the process has been executed in a real organization.

---

> **Portfolio boundary:** Duckworks, Project W.I.N.G., its personnel, systems, datasets, decisions, controls, and evidence are fictional or synthetic unless a file explicitly identifies a public source. Folder descriptions explain the intended governance role of the artifacts; they do not convert draft, planned, or template material into implemented controls, legal compliance, certification, or independent assurance.
