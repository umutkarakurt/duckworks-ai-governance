## Purpose

Policies and standards state **what must be true**. Playbooks and runbooks describe **what responsible teams do when a governance event occurs**.

The operational playbook pack is designed around triggers such as intake, assessment, third-party review, security review, human oversight, release, incidents, monitoring, significant change, retirement, and assurance.

Each operational action should make clear:

- what triggered the workflow;
- who leads;
- required sequence and specialist handoffs;
- blocking conditions;
- evidence to retain;
- escalation and stop-use points;
- when reassessment is required.

## Boundary

These playbooks are Duckworks organizational procedures. They do not determine legal classification, create external legal obligations, or prove NIST/ISO conformity.

A designed runbook is not operating evidence. Completed tickets, decisions, logs, test results, approvals, incident records, or other execution evidence would be required to prove operation.
