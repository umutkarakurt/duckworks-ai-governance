This folder contains the principal policy layer beneath the Enterprise AI Governance Charter.

## Current artifacts

- `Duckworks_AI_Governance_Policy_v1.0.docx` — enterprise requirements for accountable, risk-based, secure, transparent, and auditable AI governance across the lifecycle.
- `Duckworks_AI_Responsible_Use_Policy_v1.0.docx` — day-to-day behavioral and responsible-use requirements, including approved use, material AI, public AI, shadow AI, human oversight, and material change concepts.
- `Duckworks_AI_Whistleblower_Policy_v1.0.docx` — AI-specific overlay for confidential reporting, anti-retaliation, independent handling, and routing of serious AI governance concerns.

## Key policy distinction

The Governance Policy explicitly separates:

- binding law;
- Duckworks governance authority and internal requirements;
- voluntary standards/framework guidance;
- project assumptions.

The Responsible Use Policy governs user and team behavior rather than replacing system-level approval.

The Whistleblower Policy is designed as an AI-specific overlay to an enterprise speak-up process, not as a claim that Duckworks should create a duplicate AI-only hotline.

## Evidence expectation

Policy publication alone does not prove implementation. Approval, communication, training, acknowledgements, exceptions, enforcement, and operating records would be needed to demonstrate effectiveness in a real environment.
