## 6. Internal Risk Ratings Are Not Legal Classifications

Duckworks uses internal enterprise risk ratings such as:

- Low;
- Moderate;
- High;
- Critical.

These ratings are governance decisions used to support prioritization, treatment, approval, escalation, and monitoring.

They must not be confused with statutory concepts such as an **EU AI Act high-risk AI system**.

Legal classification and internal enterprise risk classification are separate analyses.

A system may be internally High or Critical for reasons such as safety, confidentiality, operational dependency, or fairness even where no legal high-risk classification has been established.

Similarly, a legally significant system cannot be treated as acceptable merely because an internal numerical risk score is low.
