This folder provides the primary system-level inventory for the six registered Duckworks use cases, with separate treatment of shadow AI.

- `Duckworks_Synthetic_AI_System_Inventory_v1.0.xlsx` — structured working register.
- `Duckworks_Synthetic_AI_System_Inventory_v1.0.csv` — CSV-ready representation.

- `Duckworks_Synthetic_AI_System_Inventory_Report_v1.0.pdf` — formatted review copy.

## Scope

The synthetic inventory covers **AI-001 through AI-006**. AI-007 Unregistered GenAI Usage is intentionally excluded from the six-system governed inventory because it represents an uncontrolled organizational condition that must first be discovered and decomposed into individual use cases.

## Key fields demonstrated

Intended purpose, business and technical ownership, lifecycle stage, AI technology, users, affected parties, data categories, third-party involvement, human oversight, risk/impact indicators, assessment status, approval gate, monitoring requirements, and assumption IDs.

## Important interpretation

Moderate/High/Critical indicators in the inventory are internal governance labels. They are not statutory AI classifications.

Where the inventory introduces fictional implementation details not established by the original scenario, those details should remain linked to explicit assumptions rather than being presented as verified facts.
