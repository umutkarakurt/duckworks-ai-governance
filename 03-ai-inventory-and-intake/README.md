This folder establishes enterprise visibility of AI use and the technical assets that support it.

## Subfolders

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `01-system-inventory/` | Governed AI system/use-case inventory and portfolio-level ownership/lifecycle information. |
| `02-technical-asset-registers/` | Technical AI asset inventory and AI Bill of Materials (AI BOM) register. |

## Why the distinction matters

The **AI system inventory** answers governance questions such as: What is the intended purpose? Who owns the use? Who is affected? What is the lifecycle gate? What data and third parties are involved?

The **asset inventory / AI BOM** supports deeper technical traceability into components, models, services, libraries, data dependencies, or other assets.

These layers should remain related but not collapsed into one undifferentiated register.

## Governance rule

Registration is not approval. Inclusion in an inventory does not mean that a system is approved for unrestricted production, legally compliant, or adequately controlled.

Material changes to purpose, model/provider, data, affected population, integrations, permissions, geography, or autonomy should update the relevant inventory record and may trigger reassessment.
