## Important boundary

This is an **internal Duckworks adaptation**, not an official NIST template.

The portfolio does not claim:

- real human-subject testing;
- production AI evaluation;
- vendor evaluation;
- NIST participation, review, or endorsement;
- certification or conformity assessment.

ARIA and the NIST AI RMF are treated as voluntary guidance/research resources. They do not create EU legal obligations or establish compliance with the EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001, or other regimes.

Use this folder as evidence of evaluation-method design and documentation discipline, not as proof that a production evaluation has been executed.
