## Review perspectives

The review challenges the portfolio through lenses such as:

- AI governance hiring manager;
- Internal Audit;
- CISO / AI security;
- data protection;
- AI product ownership.

## Findings focus

The findings register is designed to surface unsupported assumptions, inconsistent or overly precise risk scoring, weak control evidence, ownership conflicts, terminology collisions, framework-mapping limitations, and other issues likely to be challenged in an interview or assurance review.

The register includes structured fields such as finding ID, severity, perspectives, theme, claim status, observation, evidence source, likely challenge, remediation need, priority, and portfolio-blocker status.

## How to use this folder

Do not treat skeptical findings as a defect to hide. They are evidence that the portfolio can identify and manage its own weaknesses.

The next maturity step is issue lifecycle management: owner → management response → due date → status → closure evidence → independent validation where appropriate.
