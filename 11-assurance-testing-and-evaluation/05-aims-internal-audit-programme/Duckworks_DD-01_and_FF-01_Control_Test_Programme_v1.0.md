# DD-01 and FF-01 Control Test Programme

Engagement: AUD-AIMS-2026-001  
Version: 1.0  
Method: 100% test of controls carrying an unsupported `Implemented` source label

## Common test sequence

1. Confirm the control objective, scope, criteria and approved design.
2. Confirm an accountable owner is assigned.
3. Retrieve stable evidence IDs supporting implementation.
4. establish the complete operating population and review period.
5. Inspect a risk-based sample of version-bound execution records.
6. Inspect exceptions, rejections, overrides and escalations, including credible zero-event attestations.
7. Reperform or recalculate relevant metrics and inspect dated owner review.
8. distinguish design, implementation, operation and outcome effectiveness.
9. conclude whether the source status is supported and whether risk credit is justified.

## Retest and closure

Retest requires all requested evidence or an approved status downgrade plus risk reassessment and crosswalk update. Closure requires an independent human validator. The executable gate may confirm structural completeness but cannot close either finding.
