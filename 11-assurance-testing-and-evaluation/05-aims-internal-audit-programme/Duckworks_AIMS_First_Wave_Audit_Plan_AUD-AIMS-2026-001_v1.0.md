# First-Wave AIMS Audit Plan — AUD-AIMS-2026-001

Engagement: Implemented-claim substantiation  
Version: 1.0  
Review date: 2026-09-09  
Engagement lead: Exercise analyst; formal assignment pending  
Report approver: Head of Internal Audit / independent competent designee

## Objective

Determine whether repository evidence substantiates the `Implemented` source status of `DD-01 Competent Engineer Approval` and `FF-01 Human Planning Approval & Override`, and whether either control can receive implementation, operating-effectiveness or risk-reduction credit.

## Scope and criteria

- Population: both canonical controls with `Implemented` source status and no linked evidence in the approved v1.6 applicability register.
- Period: repository evidence available through 2026-09-09.
- Criteria: the v1.6 applicability method and register; the v1.4 control framework report; the v1.6 evidence index; the evidence requirements in this audit procedure.
- Coverage: 100% of the two-control population; no statistical sampling.
- Exclusions: production systems, private operational repositories, interviews, external confirmations and evidence not supplied in the reviewed repository.

## Procedures

For each control, confirm design and owner; search stable evidence links; request the population, period, execution records, exceptions/overrides, metrics and owner review; inspect/reperform a sample if supplied; compare evidence to the source status; and document limitations and conclusion.

## Planned communications

| Event | Audience | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Opening notice and evidence request | Control owners, AI Governance Lead | 2026-09-09 |
| Evidence response deadline | Control owners | 2026-09-16 |
| Draft finding validation | Control owner and independent audit reviewer | After evidence receipt or deadline |
| Final report and escalation | CRCO and AIMS governance forum | By 2026-10-31 |

## Result

The reviewed repository contains design assertions and named owners but no linked stable evidence ID or operating evidence for either control. Both implementation claims are therefore **not demonstrated**. `IAF-2026-002` and `IAF-2026-003` remain open pending evidence and management response.

The result does not prove the controls did not operate; it establishes that the current repository cannot support the `Implemented` labels or any effectiveness/risk-reduction claim.

## Authorization and limitations

The work is a synthetic portfolio assurance exercise. Auditor identity, competence, organizational independence and management approval are pending. Formal internal-audit reliance requires an authorized independent reviewer to validate the workpapers and approve or revise the conclusions.
