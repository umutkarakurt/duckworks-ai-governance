## Resource and safeguard requirements

- Assign an audit lead independent of the control owners and evidence preparers.
- Verify audit competence plus relevant AI, engineering, supply-chain, security, privacy or legal expertise before work begins.
- Use subject-matter specialists as advisers without transferring audit judgment.
- Record and resolve conflicts before fieldwork.
- Escalate inability to obtain evidence or resources to the CRCO.

## Change control

The programme is reviewed quarterly and after material triggers. Schedule changes must retain complete universe coverage and be approved by the audit owner and CRCO. An engagement may be accelerated; it may not be silently removed.
