# Executive Case Study

**Repository path:** `00-executive-case-study/`  
**Status:** Portfolio baseline / recommended starting point  

[← Back to main portfolio](../README.md)

This folder is the fastest entry point for reviewers who want to understand the **business case, governance problem, current portfolio position, and recommended response** before opening detailed workpapers.

## What this folder demonstrates

- translation of AI adoption into a business and governance problem;
- executive-level communication of risk, value, and release constraints;
- risk-proportionate governance rather than blanket restriction;
- separation of business-value hypotheses from unsupported ROI claims;
- ability to summarize a multi-artifact governance program for senior decision-makers.

## Current artifacts

- `Duckworks AI Governance Readiness Assessment.pdf` — executive case study covering the seven-entry baseline, major governance findings, risk concentration, and target governance response.
- `Duckworks_AI_Governance_Business_Case_v1.0.md` / source DOCX — business case for enterprise, risk-based AI governance. The business case intentionally does **not** claim quantified ROI because reliable cost and benefit baselines are not established.

## Key current decisions

The portfolio uses differentiated governance gates. DuckTalent AI and Unregistered GenAI Usage remain the most restrictive cases; FeatherForecast is the comparatively lower-impact operational baseline; several other systems remain restricted pilots or blocked pending evidence.

These are **Duckworks internal governance decisions based on fictional assumptions**, not legal determinations.

## Suggested review order

1. Read the Readiness Assessment.
2. Read the Business Case.
3. Continue to `01-project-charter-and-context/`.
4. Follow the evidence chain through inventory, risk, impact, controls, and assurance.
