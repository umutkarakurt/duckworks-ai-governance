# Project Charter and Context

**Repository path:** `01-project-charter-and-context/`  
**Status:** Portfolio baseline  

[← Back to main portfolio](../README.md)

This folder establishes the **controlled context** for Project W.I.N.G. before risk scores, legal conclusions, controls, or deployment decisions are considered.

The project deliberately treats scenario facts, assumptions, scope boundaries, stakeholders, and acceptance criteria as governance inputs rather than background narrative. This prevents later assessments from silently relying on unstated conditions.

## Subfolders

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `01-organization-and-scenario/` | Defines Duckworks, Project W.I.N.G., business drivers, the seven-entry AI portfolio, and the governance problem. |
| `02-objectives-and-scope/` | Defines project objectives, in-scope and out-of-scope activities, and the intended assurance boundary. |
| `03-project-governance/` | Records stakeholders, assumptions, required deliverables, and measurable acceptance criteria. |

## Governance significance

These artifacts are upstream dependencies for the rest of the portfolio. A material change to intended purpose, human authority, geography, product scope, data use, or legal role may invalidate later conclusions and trigger reassessment.

A reviewer should use this section to answer:

- What problem is the project solving?
- What does the project claim to cover?
- Which facts are fictional decisions and which remain open assumptions?
- Who owns, challenges, approves, and independently assures the program?
- What evidence is required before the project can be considered complete?

---

> **Portfolio boundary:** Duckworks, Project W.I.N.G., its personnel, systems, datasets, decisions, controls, and evidence are fictional or synthetic unless a file explicitly identifies a public source. Folder descriptions explain the intended governance role of the artifacts; they do not convert draft, planned, or template material into implemented controls, legal compliance, certification, or independent assurance.
