## 1. Evidence-state rules

| Evidence state | Meaning | Production-effectiveness use |
|---|---|---|
| Designed / designed test input | Design, procedure, test data or expected evidence is documented. | Not permitted |
| Synthetic implementation or execution demonstrated | A bounded fictional implementation, workflow, failure or test has been executed. | Not permitted |
| Synthetic operation tested | The control operated across the stated synthetic population and was tested. | Not permitted |
| Synthetic lifecycle decision or reassessment demonstrated | A bounded governance decision/change/reassessment record exists. | Not permitted |
| Production operating evidence | Version-bound real-world execution over a defined period, with population, exceptions and review. | Eligible for assessment; not automatically effective |
| Validated effectiveness | Sustained outcomes and operation have been competently challenged and accepted for the defined scope. | May support approved risk reduction |
| Not available | Required evidence is identified but not present or retrievable. | Not permitted |

## 5. Index maintenance procedure

1. Assign a stable evidence ID before an artifact is used in a decision.
2. Record the exact AI, risk and control IDs; artifact name and location; owner; system/control version; population; period; state; limitations and reviewer.
3. Record whether evidence is synthetic or production-derived. Never infer this from the filename.
4. Link evidence to the decision it informed without implying that evidence automatically changed a score.
5. When evidence is superseded, preserve the old record, identify the successor and update affected risk, control, crosswalk and management records.
6. Test retrieval and cross-document consistency at least quarterly and after a material repository change.

## 6. Current decision boundary

The evidence in this index does not relax any existing lifecycle gate. Synthetic artifacts remain suitable for portfolio demonstration and control-design challenge, but they are not substitutes for defined-period production operation, outcome monitoring or independent validation.
